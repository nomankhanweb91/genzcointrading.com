Funding Rate

Periodic payments exchanged directly between long and short futures traders to keep perpetual contract prices aligned with spot index prices.

Advertisement

In-Depth Explanation

When funding is positive, longs pay shorts (bullish bias). When negative, shorts pay longs (bearish bias).

Practical Market Example

Real-World Application

A +0.01% funding rate paid every 8 hours on a perpetual futures position.
